ABOUT THE PROJECT

90's Hip Hop is a curated archive exploring the music that defined hip-hop's golden era from 1990 to 1999.

HOW THE TRACKS WERE SELECTED

This is not a Billboard chart or a ranking based only on record sales.

Each track was selected by considering:

  • Cultural impact
  • Influence on hip-hop
  • Production and sound
  • Lyrical significance
  • Regional and scene importance
  • How strongly the track represents '90s Hip-Hop

Commercial success was considered, but a major hit was not automatically ranked above a less commercially successful track.

The yearly Top 10 lists were curated to represent different artists, sounds, regions, and scenes. When possible, one representative track per artist was selected for each year.

The Essential Top 10 was then selected from the yearly lists to represent the decade as a whole.

THE ARCHIVE

10 YEARS. 200 TRACKS.

100 tracks from the East Coast collection and 100 tracks from the West Coast collection

Each year features a curated Top 10, followed by the Essential Top 10 representing the decade as a whole.
